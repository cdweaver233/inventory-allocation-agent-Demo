The Algorithm

How the agent scores every order

Three weighted inputs. One priority score. Highest score gets fulfilled first.

40%
Penalty Risk
1–10 scale
Risk of SLA breach or retailer chargeback if the order isn't fulfilled on time.
35%
Margin
1–10 scale
How much profit Citizen earns per unit sold — the more profitable the SKU, the higher it scores.
25%
Strategic Tier
T1=10 · T2=6 · T3=3
Importance of the account to Citizen's long-term strategy — T1 includes key accounts like Tourneau and Nordstrom.
Priority Score = (Margin × 0.35) + (Penalty Risk × 0.40) + (Tier × 0.25)
